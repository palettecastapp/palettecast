About PaletteCast

PaletteCast is designed to inspire and challenge. Are you stuck in a rut — always reaching for the same favorite colors? Do you want to push yourself with techniques you usually shy away from? Or do you just want the whimsical serendipity of the unexpected, and to remove the pressure of choosing from your creative session? That's what PaletteCast is for — artists like you.

One thing to know: PaletteCast is made for pourers who already know their way around a cup and canvas. We're not covering the basics of viscosity, medium ratios, or materials here — that's a whole world of its own, and there are amazing creators out there who do it beautifully. We're just here to help inspire a little magic. .
