Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

PaletteCast ("the App") is developed by Bethany Foster, an independent developer based in Greeneville, Tennessee, USA ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. No Personal Data Collection

PaletteCast does not require an account, login, or registration of any kind. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location. The App's color palettes, settings, and other content are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PaletteCast displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) and other technical information to serve ads and personalize them based on your interests. This data collection and processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies, not by us directly.

You can learn more about how Google uses this information and manage your ad personalization settings here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

PaletteCast offers an optional one-time "Pro" upgrade that unlocks additional features. This purchase is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information, such as credit card numbers. Google's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, other than the limited technical information automatically processed by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing as described above.

5. Children's Privacy

PaletteCast is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
